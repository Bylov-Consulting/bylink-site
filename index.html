Bylink apps for Shopify

Small, focused extensions for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central that build on Microsoft's own Shopify Connector. Each app solves one job that the connector leaves to you, and each one is switched on per shop.

The apps

Bylink Order Sync for Shopify

Keeps Business Central sales orders in step with cancelled and edited Shopify orders.

How it works

Bylink Return Handling for Shopify

Turns Shopify returns and refunds into return orders and credit memos, and sends the refund back to Shopify.

How it works

Bylink Pre-Orders for Shopify

Publishes pre-order status and expected dates from Business Central to Shopify products.

How it works

Bylink Tag Order Routing for Shopify

Sends Shopify orders to a fixed customer by order tag and ship-to country.

How it works

How they fit together

Every Bylink app installs on top of Microsoft's Shopify Connector, and brings along the shared library apps it needs: Bylink Shopify Admin API, which talks to the Shopify Admin API with your shop's credentials, and Bylink Shopify Feature Flags, which lets you switch features on or off per shop. You do not buy the libraries separately. See Getting started.

Get the apps

The apps are sold through Microsoft AppSource. Each app costs EUR 20, USD 20 or DKK 149 per user per month, with a one-month free trial. Billing is handled by Microsoft. Use in sandbox environments is free.
