Privacy statement

Applies to all Bylink apps for Shopify. Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Who we are

Bylov Consulting ApS, Buskelundengen 128, 8600 Silkeborg, Denmark, CVR 43849972, publishing as Bylov Consulting ("we", "us"), publishes the Bylink apps for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. For questions about this statement or your personal data, write to support@bylov.net.

The short version

  • The apps run inside your Business Central environment. The business data they work with (orders, customers, items, returns) stays in your Business Central and your Shopify store. We do not receive a copy of it.
  • The apps talk to Shopify directly from your Business Central, using credentials you set up. Nothing passes through servers of ours.
  • The apps send technical usage and error telemetry to us, so that we can support you and fix faults. It is designed to contain no names, e-mail addresses or postal addresses.
  • When you try or buy an app on AppSource, Microsoft shares your contact details with us as the publisher.

Data sent to Shopify

The apps send data to your Shopify store using your own credentials: status values written as metafields on orders and products, refund requests for received returns, and reads of order and address data. This data goes from your Business Central to Shopify and is covered by your agreement with Shopify.

Telemetry we receive

The apps send telemetry to an Azure Application Insights resource that we control, hosted by Microsoft in the EU (North Europe region). Business Central also sends its standard extension telemetry for our apps to the same resource. It contains:

  • your Business Central tenant and environment identifiers, company name and app version;
  • event identifiers, timings, counts and outcomes (for example "order routed" or "refund requested");
  • Shopify object identifiers such as order, return or product ids, and Business Central document numbers;
  • error messages raised while the apps ran.

The apps are built so that telemetry does not contain names, e-mail addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers or order contents. An error message raised by Business Central could, rarely, quote a value from the record it failed on.

Why: to support you, find and fix defects, and understand which features are used. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and improving the apps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
How long: telemetry is deleted after 90 days.
Who else: Microsoft hosts the telemetry as our processor.

Data we receive from Microsoft AppSource

When you install a trial or buy a subscription, Microsoft shares lead and order data with us: your name, company, e-mail address, phone number, country and the offer you chose. Microsoft handles billing and payment; we do not receive card or bank details. We use this data to administer your subscription, to contact you about your trial or purchase, and to provide support. We do not send you newsletters or other marketing e-mail unless you have agreed to it. Our legal basis is the performance of the contract with you and our legitimate interest in following up on a trial (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). We keep it for as long as you are a customer and for five years after the end of the financial year in which the customer relationship ends, as the Danish Bookkeeping Act requires. Microsoft's handling of this data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Support

If you contact us for support, we process your contact details and what you send us to answer you. If solving the case needs access to your Business Central or Shopify, we only get that access when you grant it, and only for that case. When we process personal data on your behalf in that work, we act as your processor and do so only under a data processing agreement with you.

Transfers outside the EU

We store telemetry and customer records in the EU. Microsoft may process AppSource data outside the EU under its own safeguards, as described in its privacy statement.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to restrict processing and to data portability. Write to support@bylov.net. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, or to the authority in your own country.

For data the apps process inside your Business Central and Shopify, you are the controller: requests from your own customers are handled with the standard tools in Business Central and Shopify.

Changes

We will update this statement when the apps or our practices change, and change the date at the top.
