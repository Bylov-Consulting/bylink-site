Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests for the Bylink apps for Shopify.

Contact

E-mail support@bylov.net. We read support e-mail on Danish business days, 09:00–16:00 CET, and aim to answer within one business day. This is a target, not a guarantee: support is provided on a best-effort basis with no service level, as set out in the license terms.

What support covers

Support covers questions about the apps' own functions and faults in them. It does not cover setting up or running Business Central, Microsoft's Shopify Connector or your Shopify store, data corrections in your environment, or consulting. Help with those can be agreed separately.

Please include

  • The app and version (Business Central: Extension Management).
  • Your Business Central environment name and whether it is a sandbox or production.
  • The Shopify shop code, and the Shopify order, return or product id if it concerns one.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened, including the error text.

Do not send passwords, API tokens or customer data by e-mail.

Before you write

Most questions are answered in the help pages. Problems with Microsoft's Shopify Connector itself go to your Business Central partner or Microsoft.
